- 2026-09-02 — Mobile horizontal-scroll bug, found and killed after five deploys. A reporting phone was panning every page sideways; the on-device probe returned **clientWidth 411 against innerWidth 876** with 465px of scroll, exactly the difference. Four separate causes, all real: carousel arrow buttons revealed below `sm` with no display class (a 6px overflow); the floating WhatsApp button and privacy notice anchored with `right-6`/`inset-x-0`, which a fixed box resolves against the *window* rather than the layout viewport, parking the button at x=852; a `sr-only` span (position:absolute, no positioned ancestor) escaping the `overflow-x-auto` wrapper on the new RTA table links; and finally the carousel tracks themselves, whose removal collapsed the window/viewport mismatch outright. `overflow-x: clip` on the root was tried and reverted — it does not clip fixed-position boxes and would only have masked the next bug. **Knowledge Corner carousels are now a plain 3-up grid** and `Carousel.astro` plus its script are deleted. Also fixed in the same pass: "Show more" appearing on every card (Tailwind's preflight `[hidden]` rule loses to the `inline-flex` utility, so the button was never hidden and the overflow measurement decided nothing). New wiki page: mobile-viewport-pitfalls.md — read it before adding anything that floats, clips or scrolls sideways.
