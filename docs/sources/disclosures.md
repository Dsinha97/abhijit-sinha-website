**Section 2: Operating Model & Remuneration Architecture**

- **Section Headline:** How Mutual Fund Distribution Remuneration Works
    
- **Core Bullet Points:**
    
    - **100% Trail-Only Structure:** Remuneration is paid solely via trail commissions disbursed by respective Asset Management Companies (AMCs) out of the scheme’s Total Expense Ratio (TER). No upfront commissions, advisory fees, or platform handling charges are levied directly on the investor.
        
    - **Regular Plan Execution:** All transactions facilitated under ARN-367596 are executed in **Regular Plans**. Investors have the option to invest directly with AMCs in **Direct Plans**, which carry lower expense ratios due to the absence of distribution commissions.
        
    - **Independent Allocation:** Fund categories and scheme suggestions are determined strictly by investor suitability, investment horizon, and risk tolerance. Commission differentials across AMCs or asset classes do not influence scheme selection.
        
    - **Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) Verification:** The exact gross commission paid to the distributor for your specific portfolio is transparently reported on your monthly or half-yearly Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) issued by depositories (NSDL/CDSL) or RTAs (CAMS/KFintech).
